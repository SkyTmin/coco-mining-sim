# Prompt: candy pickaxes, 4 families × 4 rarities (for Codex image generation)

Owner 11.11: fighter style rejected («кирки похожи друг на друга»), back to the candy style, every pickaxe must be unique.
Make FOUR images, one per family, each with 4 pickaxes in a row (common → rare → epic → legendary). Save them to
`docs/design/mockups/pickaxes-candy-ref-<family>.png` (storm, dynamite, rift, frost).

## Shared style (put at the top of every image request)

Game item concept sheet for an original Roblox mining game, "candy toy" style. Four pickaxes in one row on a flat plain
cream background (#FFF8EC), equal spacing, each pickaxe shown at the same 3/4 angle, head up-right, handle down-left,
whole item visible, no cropping. Chunky, plump, glossy toy-like shapes, like hard candy, gummy, caramel and glaze, with
soft highlights. Thick dark navy outline (#1E2140) around every item. Bold saturated colours, simple readable silhouette
at small icon size. Shapes must be buildable from simple blocky parts (boxes, cylinders, spheres, wedges): no thin
wires, no tiny details, no fur, no realistic textures. The FOUR pickaxes in the row must have clearly DIFFERENT
silhouettes, not just different colours: rarity changes the head shape and adds new parts.
Rarity ladder, same idea for every family:
- Common: cheap snack materials (wooden popsicle stick, biscuit, pretzel), one simple shape, no glow.
- Rare: sugar-coated silver metal bands and one round blue gem candy, cleaner shape, two-tone stripes.
- Epic: translucent glowing crystal candy (rock candy / lollipop glass), extra pieces floating or sticking out, soft glow.
- Legendary: gold foil and gold rims, the most dramatic silhouette, sparkles and floating particles around it.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no characters, nothing copied from existing games.

## Storm (Гроза) — family colour ice blue #8BE1FF, lightning
Shape language: zigzag lightning bolts and cotton-candy clouds.
1. Common: wooden popsicle-stick handle; the head is one flat zigzag lightning bolt of blue raspberry gummy; a small white marshmallow cloud on the joint.
2. Rare: blue-and-white striped candy-cane handle; the head is a double zigzag bolt with silver sugar-coated edges; a round blue gem candy in the centre.
3. Epic: the head is translucent blue-violet rock-candy crystal shaped as a bolt; a fluffy cotton-candy cloud on the joint with small glowing bolts sticking out of it.
4. Legendary: a gold-foil cloud head with THREE branching lightning prongs (a trident-like pick); a small glowing ball of lightning gumball orbiting; golden sprinkles in the air.

## Dynamite (Динамит) — family colour red-orange #FF5A2A, explosions
Shape language: candy sticks in wrappers, round bomb gumballs, fuses with sparks.
1. Common: pretzel-stick handle; the head is ONE red candy stick wrapped like a bonbon (twisted wrapper ends are the two points), tied across with tape; a short fuse on top.
2. Rare: the head is a BUNDLE of three red-and-white striped candy sticks held by a silver band; a round black-cherry bomb gumball on one end.
3. Epic: the head is a big round translucent cherry bomb gumball on one side and a sharp crystal-candy spike on the other; a sparkling fuse; glowing red core.
4. Legendary: the head is a giant gold-wrapped bonbon whose twisted gold-foil ends form the pick points; a sparkler fuse with a small firework burst; gold ribbons.

## Rift (Разлом) — family colour amber #FFC97A, cracks going deep
Shape language: broken toffee and caramel slabs, glowing cracks, split pieces.
1. Common: chocolate-bar handle; the head is one chunky broken slab of caramel toffee with uneven edges and a thin glowing amber crack line.
2. Rare: the head is two toffee shards held together by a silver clamp, an orange glowing gap between them; caramel drizzle drops.
3. Epic: the head is translucent amber crystal shards fanning out asymmetrically, pieces floating slightly apart with glowing crack energy between them.
4. Legendary: the head is split into two gold-rimmed halves floating apart, a swirling molten gold-caramel portal between them; small drifting fragments around.

## Frost (Мороз) — family colour white-blue #C9F3FF, freezing then shattering
Shape language: popsicles, icicles, snowflakes, frosting drips.
1. Common: the whole pickaxe is an ice lolly: two rounded pale-blue popsicles crossed on a wooden stick.
2. Rare: the head is a row of icicle spikes with a silver frost band; a round mint candy in the centre.
3. Epic: the head is translucent ice crystal; a snowflake lollipop in the centre; white frosting drips; soft cold glow.
4. Legendary: a crown of tall snowflake crystals with gold rims as the head; swirling sparkling snow around it.
