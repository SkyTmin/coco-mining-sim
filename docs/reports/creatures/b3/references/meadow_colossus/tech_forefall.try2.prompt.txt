POSES
1 REST. All four feet on the floor; nose gently forward, three-quarter cheek and amber eye visible. Belly a little above knees; tree upright.
2 LOAD. Hind feet stay at the same two ground points. Shell shifts back and rises about 28 degrees around them. Both forefeet draw in and rise to shoulder height; visible magenta crescent below both. Neck extends and looks at clear floor ahead.
3 RELEASE. Maximum rear-up, front rim nearly a head higher, shell incline 36 degrees. BOTH forelegs now unbend aggressively downward, enough ground gap under both claws (10% of standing height); back feet anchor weight. Beak and eyes look down at landing aims. No fully airborne turtle.
4 CONTACT. Shell pitches steeply forward, head reaches out and lower, both forefeet visibly TOUCH the same implicit ground plane ahead of their original footprints at full downward extension with visible separated bottoms. Three-quarter depth offset means the farther forefoot is slightly higher onscreen. Hind feet still anchored. Shell now lowest at its front edge and tree bent forward. No dust.
5 ABSORB. The front column knees yield; chest drops between shoulders while the two foot soles stay precisely where they landed. Shoulders rounded, nose a little raised, hill rebounds slightly, trunk hangs forward.
6 RECOVER. Four feet back at regular stance; shell level again; neck eases to neutral and tree has a very slight opposite lean. Same size and floor as opening.
