REFERENCE SHEET — Meadow Colossus, animation "trick". The tortoise rolls its front pebble along the beak, tosses it back and catches it with its own grassy shell.

LAYOUT
One wide 1536×1024 image divided invisibly into three columns and two rows of six equal 512×512 cells. Read top left to bottom right. Six drawings only. Fill all background with perfectly flat solid #FF00FF. No cast shadows, ground, scenery, vignette, grids, strokes across gutters, text, digits, labels, arrows, logos, or captions. Separate six fully contained, horizontally centred figures, at least 8% clear margin on EVERY side per cell. Standing creature takes 66% cell height; invisible baseline under its near feet at 84%, camera remains tripod-fixed across all cells.

CHARACTER LOCK
Use ONLY the LEFT three-quarter model in the attached identity reference: one ancient wide, low tortoise, one domed shell, ochre angular scute rim, olive grassy meadow growing on top, three stones in the same order (two angular bedrocks next to the one crooked tree, one smaller rounded loose pebble at the FRONT lip). One tree with three rounded crowns; its visible roots cling to the shell. Four short stocky column legs, three blunt ochre claws per foot including near hind, two amber eyes (far eye partly occluded), one cream hooked beak without teeth, one pointed short tail projecting behind the shell. Keep leg identities and overlap consistent. Total standing height 100 units, shell body before tree 58, head height 22, legs 24. Locked colours olive moss #86982c, rim ochre #d49c4f, shadowed earth #715432, granite #988b6b, near-black brown outline #30291d, eyes #e5a03b. Light upper left. Simplify texture into readable hard-edged pixel clusters while preserving the character. No extra stones, feet, tree or animals.

CAMERA AND STYLE
Static front-right 3/4 view at ~35° elevation, turtle facing screen right; see its near cheek continuously, far forefoot at extreme right and near forefoot lower and more central. Pixel-art cel shading: dark single-pixel contours, four discrete light values, absolutely crisp transitions to magenta. It is ONE image with SIX different acting beats, never two turnarounds. No motion blur, FX, debris or ground lines. The animation will add effects from physical contacts later.

AVOID
Extra limbs, fingers, claws, trees, rocks, stray particles, heads hidden by props, floating feet on contacts, fused front and hind feet, generic brown turtles without the meadow, fake shadows, outlines around cells, cropping, anti-alias fringing, copying the right-hand lateral view from the identity reference.

PROP: exactly ONE smooth round tan-gray pebble, diameter 8% standing height, stable proportions and size; TWO bigger fixed split boulders remain in their usual positions. The same sole loose pebble leaves its resting depression on the front shoulder of the hill, travels outside the shell and settles back into that depression. Keep round rocker visible against a contrasting patch. At the apex put it IN FRONT of the dark tree outline above the grassy rim, so its outline reads while the full-body drawing remains one connected silhouette. Never pass it in front of either eye.
POSES
1 REST. Four legs planted. Small round pebble on front grass lip nearest the beak; one crooked tree and two heavier grey rocks behind. Gaze neutral toward screen right.
2 ROLL. Both foreknees lower as one. Shell tips downward toward the right. The one rounded pebble rolls over the golden rim to touch the upturned upper edge of the beak, in front of the eye; leave no second stone in the empty cleft. Snout points under pebble.
3 TOSS. Now the nose sweeps up toward the brow: direct contact under the pebble, eyes follow it, mouth closed. Neck extended, chest rises tall. Rock is directly above the tip of the beak and its outline touches only the nose; shell still clear of it.
4 APEX. Stone has risen and travelled backwards a short distance, now crossing in front of the tree's dark trunk or lowest green canopy above the shell. Keep the one smooth stone with a crisp bright rim visibly in front of that contrasting dark foliage. Snout follows up and backward, the amber eye watches against the sky. All four feet remain stable.
5 CATCH / TA-DA. Same small round stone visibly seated back into the recognizable FRONT notch of the grassy shell; grass under it compresses. The front knees yield, eye curved with pleasure, tongue stays inside beak. Let tree dip slightly.
6 RETURN. Body rises to even stance, beak returns level, pleased eye half-open, roots and crowns settle and all three rocks are accounted for. Four same paws, same floor and size as cell one.
